# Whole-span context delivery (market-v0.3.6)

This market-only adapter retains the pinned development engine and the unchanged
search, body-capture and forecast implementations. Its separate runtime digest
is recorded in deployment and projection receipts. Competition controls are not
changed.

The native 28,000-character delivery ceiling remains enforced. Normal requests
use the native projection unchanged. If its smallest reading page does not fit,
the adapter pages graph navigation: previous detailed node annotations, past
update explanations and metadata for other pending materials. Their saved full
records remain available through local inspection. Whole historical material
requests may be deferred as the last navigation fallback.

Immutable question fields, complete resolution rules, current target definitions,
gap meanings, action constraints and source identity remain protected. Original
evidence spans are delivered whole or explicitly omitted; their text, IDs,
coordinates and hashes are never shortened or substituted. An unfit immutable
objective or indivisible source span still fails locally without a provider call.
Projection receipts record preparation, not successful delivery, processing,
comprehension or truth. The native provider journal retains actual calls.

The raw handoff also projects out only hash-verified historical `ProviderCap`
receipts that did not transmit. This applies to the existing local-context-error
raw-export gate. Actual service failures and unknown reservations remain charged
and cannot pass that gate. All original collector files and results remain intact;
the handoff retains the incomplete result and does not claim complete evidence.

Validation replays Anthropic, Bronson and Taylor against exactly the archived
originals, across five dispatch phases each. It verifies the message ceiling,
literal span bindings, unchanged rule fields and raw handoff without model/search
calls. Live forecasting completion is checked separately after deployment. The
daily 1500, task 60, dispatch 200 and existing search ceilings stay unchanged.
