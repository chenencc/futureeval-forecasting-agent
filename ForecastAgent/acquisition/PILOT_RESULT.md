## Remaining limitations

- Unattempted saved source leads do not uniformly trigger independent repair.
- The 60000-character review reading budget omits 151 saved units in question
  43494 and 42 in question 36871. Some official material is captured but unread.
- Long tracking URLs dominate one regional AAA body; their contribution to
  Mercury's repeated service rejection is a hypothesis.
- The CMC historical body explicitly identifies June 30 and preserves both
  target coin rows. Mercury nevertheless selects a current USDC source for a
  different field. Original `by` versus paraphrased `as-of` conflicts remain.
- The frozen Instructure fixture omits the opening timestamp required by its
  after-opening condition.
- Raw source-budget stop reasons and unread candidates must remain separately
  visible even when the collector's exported `gaps` array is empty.

This pilot validates module connection and snapshot preservation. It does not
establish gap-free scalable acquisition or semantic review readiness.
