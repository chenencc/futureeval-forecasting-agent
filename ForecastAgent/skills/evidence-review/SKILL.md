name: evidence-review
description: Audit saved facts for identity, time and full-claim support.
---

Review all evidence IDs with audit_evidence. Check the full claim against its exact quote, subject identity, document stage, dates and historical availability. Reject an absence claim supported only by one observed event or failed discovery. Group republications by original source; they do not become independent corroboration. Future effective schedules may be known before cutoff only with eligible announcement evidence. Model self-review is not independent truth verification. Finish with accepted facts, unresolved gaps, conflicts and temporal caveats, never a forecast or outcome assertion.
