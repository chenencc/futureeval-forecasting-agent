description: Identify entities and resolution requirements before collecting evidence.
---

Freeze the subject identity, jurisdiction, event window and every resolution condition. Prefer sources explicitly designated by the question. Separate event time, announcement time, effective time and observation availability. Build critical needs that can be independently checked. A failed search cannot prove absence. Use plan_evidence and real source URLs; do not infer outcomes from resolved-question context.

Read useful resolution-source URLs before spending all three lifetime Tavily basic calls. First search for designated primary sources, second for independent reporting, third only for a specific remaining gap. Use general for official records, news for reporting and finance for economic data; do not restrict broad primary-source discovery to news. A current official homepage may lead to a specific release or RSS feed; use only captured links.

After a useful body is captured, use find_passages or search_saved_text and copy a short exact passage into record_quote. Alternatively copy excerpt_args unchanged and add need_ids. Never estimate character positions or save navigation as substantive material. Basic Extract is one optional paid rescue batch for important failed URLs, not another search.

Check collection_checkpoint before finishing. Consider supported official adapters and a short Polymarket entity/event query while HTTP budget remains. Store rules and display prices separately, without equivalence or edge calculations. Record concrete channel deferrals; do not spend a call merely to check a box. Acquisition gaps are missing material, not assertions that an event has not happened.
