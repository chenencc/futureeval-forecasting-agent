---
name: official-events
description: Verify corporate, government, legal and election event stages.
---

Check exact legal entity, jurisdiction, document type and date. Company-name matches alone do not identify a filer; funds and ETFs can have similar names. Separate rumors, announcements, confidential filings, public filings, passage, signature, entry into force, preliminary counts and certified results. Record the stage explicitly. A public database cannot establish absence of confidential submissions. Obtain authentic documents and cross-check reporting where the condition remains ambiguous. Do not guess government URLs, filing paths or CIKs.
