# Nonbinary full pipeline pilot

The manual `nonbinary_full_pipeline.yaml` workflow runs the frozen nonbinary core twenty through the original collection agent, the independent supplement toolchain, and Mercury conditional original-evidence rereading. It never submits forecasts or loads evaluation labels.

The prior simplified pilot (run 37027378998) supplies immutable seed captures and journals. Each task imports its consumed one Tavily basic reservation and six free fetch reservations. The existing lifetime ceilings remain three Tavily basic searches, eight acquisition fetches, and one Exa search. Snippets remain search leads, not readable body captures. Ultra is the collection model with the existing service-failure fallback to Super. Collection may resume at most three executions without resetting physical reservations.

Independent supplement uses the existing bounded HTTP, saved-byte parsing, and Playwright repair routes. Its child ledger preserves the exact acquisition parent hashes. Mercury consumes the resulting overlay and uses its existing conditional second reading only when new saved original spans are available. All stage journals are uploaded even after failure; `resume_run` restores these journals without resetting budgets.

These are current-information retrospective diagnostics. Search and article bodies may reveal resolved outcomes. Probability and local payload validity do not establish source relevance or correctness.
