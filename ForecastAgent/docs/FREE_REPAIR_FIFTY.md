# Free repair of the completed fifty

This opt-in experiment supplements the fifty completed handoffs from run
37101299893. The seventy pending recollection tasks remain stopped. Production
collection and forecasting are unchanged.

Each task retains its previous independent repair reservations and captures.
The cumulative free allowance increases from two to ten HTTP downloads and from
two to four Chromium renders. A render remains limited to 25 requests; downloads
remain limited to 8 MB and five redirects. Local reparse remains limited to eight.
Failed and interrupted reservations count toward these totals. Previously
attempted URLs are not blindly retried.

Exact rule sources precede original failed sources; generic observed alternatives
come last. Generic approval and announcement words do not establish entity
matching. Candidate discovery uses only saved URLs and makes no search calls.
The experiment makes no model, Tavily, Exa, analysis or submission calls.

The registered `supplement_campaign.yaml` workflow supports `mode=recollection50`
with `source_run=37101299893`. Ten sequential matrix jobs process five tasks each.
Every batch uploads its own state even on failure. A retry must restore the
latest matching batch artifact using `resume_run`; rerunning without restoration
is rejected. Do not dispatch a new fresh experiment over the same parent after
attempts have started. Original acquisition and repair ledgers remain auditable.

Reports distinguish added readable pages from relevance and temporal eligibility.
Unresolved authentication, paywall, missing data, PDF image and historical-period
gaps remain recorded. More free downloads cannot guarantee complete evidence.
